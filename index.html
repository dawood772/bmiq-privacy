BMIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

BMIQ (“the app”) is published by Optic Ltd.

We do not collect your data

The height and weight you type into BMIQ are used only on your device to calculate a BMI value. They are never sent to us or shared with anyone.

What is stored on your device

BMIQ keeps a history of your last 10 results and your chosen unit (imperial or metric) in the app’s private storage on your device, so you can see them next time you open the app. You can delete the history at any time with “Clear history”, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Accounts, ads and analytics

The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics, and it does not need an internet connection to work.

Permissions

BMIQ does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone. BMI categories shown in the app apply to adults (18+).

Medical disclaimer

BMIQ provides general information only and is not medical advice. Talk to a qualified health professional about your health.

Changes to this policy
